🎲 Tier Roll

Every pack rolls a rarity tier first, then a specific card inside it. Common 42% · Uncommon 35% · Rare 20% · Epic 2.5% · Legendary 0.5% — one table, used by every ordinary pack and by boosts too.

⚖️ In-Tier Weighting

Inside a tier the cards aren't equal odds — each one's weight is 1 ÷ its own power, normalized against its tier-mates. Half the power is twice the pull chance. Fruit and veggie share one pool, never split.

🌱 Garden Multiplier

A higher garden tier scales a crop's power up and its grow time down by the same factor. One harvest pays the identical total either way — a faster tier just means more harvests per week. Full table below.

Boost Items

Garden Tiers

Same Payout, Your Speed

A garden multiplies a crop's power up by its tier and divides the grow cycle down by that exact same number — one finished harvest pays the identical total at any tier. What changes is throughput: a higher tier just finishes more cycles in the same stretch of real time.

power(tier) = base power × multiplier cycle(tier) = base days ÷ multiplier
