⛏️ Player Guide

Welcome to Mother Lode

Five mining sites. Eighteen machines. One of them is on the Moon. You run a mining operation, your rigs earn a share of a fixed pool of $GREEN every second, and every rig you send underground is quietly rolling for a hidden jackpot you only see when you pull it back up.

1What Mother Lode is

Mother Lode is a mining game on the Solana blockchain. You stake a claim, get mining machines called rigs, and put them to work at one of five sites: four on Earth and one on the Moon.

The game holds a fixed pool of 200 billion $GREEN. Every day it releases 0.05% of whatever is left, and that release is shared among everyone mining. The release is split two ways:

  • 75% streams to miners continuously, by how much mining power each has at each site. This is the balance you claim.
  • 25% goes into a separate jackpot pool. Your rigs roll against it while they work, and whatever they win stays hidden until you pull the rig.

Nothing new is ever created to pay you: the pool only shrinks. The more total mining power in the game, the smaller each person's slice, so what you earn is your share of the pool, not a fixed amount per rig.

Practice demo. If you open the game without a Solana wallet installed, you get a practice demo that runs entirely in your browser with no real money. Everything in this guide describes the real game you play with a connected wallet.

3Joining the game

You need an invite

Mother Lode is invite-only: every miner joins under another miner. Enter their referral code on the "Who sent you?" screen. If you don't have one, tap 🎲 No code? Match me with a miner and you'll be shown real miners who were active in the last 12 hours, with how many people each has already referred. Pick anyone. Choosing someone with few referrals helps a smaller player.

The buy-in

Buy-in$25, paid in SOL (0.25 SOL at the game's $100-per-SOL price)
Starter $GREEN$5 of the $25 buys $GREEN for you on the open market, in the same transaction
Starter rigOne MINE IT chest, opened the moment you're in. It can be anything from a Pickaxe Crew up.
Rest of the $25Goes to the liquidity pool (and part to the treasury once the pool is deep)

Your wallet shows the exact transaction before anything moves. You'll also be asked once to sign a message. It costs nothing and proves the wallet is yours, and it lasts 24 hours so you aren't asked again for every action.

One mine per wallet. When you connect, the game checks whether your wallet already owns a mine. If it does, you go straight in. You never pay twice.

Got a gift code?

If a friend bought you a mine, enter their gift code on the invite screen (you still need a referral code too). The buy-in is already paid, so you don't need any SOL to claim it. Your gift $GREEN (and SOL, if they added it) arrives in your wallet shortly after.

4Sites and heat

There are five sites. Four are on Earth: Surface Quarry, Deep Shaft, Crystal Caverns and Abyssal Trench. The fifth is The Moon, which plays by its own rules (see The Moon).

Each Earth site has a heat level: Frozen, Cold, Steady, Hot or Blazing. Heat decides how big a slice of the Earth emissions a site pulls. A hot site pays the rigs working it more. Heat re-rolls on a random, hidden schedule, every 1 to 7 days per site. Nobody can see when the next change is coming, so it pays to check in.

Good to know. A site with no rigs on it takes no slice at all. Its share goes to the sites that are actually being mined.

Deploying

Open a site, tap rigs into its open slots (or tap an empty slot and pick a rig), then press Confirm & Sign. Staging is free and you can pull a staged rig back out before you confirm. The rig starts mining as soon as you confirm.

Rigs are one-time use. Deploying uses up the card: the rig leaves your bag and works one full cycle. When you pull it, it's gone. To keep a slot mining, you need a fresh rig for it.

When a cycle finishes

A finished rig stops earning immediately. You then have 7 days to pull it. After that it burns for good, and anything it struck burns with it. The game shows a countdown and warns you when a rig has less than a day left.

6Pulling a rig

Pulling ends the run, consumes the rig and pays out whatever it struck. How much you keep depends on how far through its cycle it was:

Progress when pulledWhat happens to its jackpot winnings
Under 73.3%All forfeited and burned.
73.3% to 100%A sliding penalty: about 80% lost just past 73.3%, easing to 0% at 100%. For example, about 60% lost at 80%, 30% at 90%, 15% at 95%.
100% (finished)You keep it all.

Winnings go straight to your wallet, usually within about 15 seconds. They never wait for the 3-hour claim timer, and a raid can never touch them. The referral cut (see Referrals) is taken from them first.

Pull bonus

Every pull that isn't a forfeit (73.3% or more) rolls once for a free chest: 30% a MINE IT chest, 30% a MYSTERY chest, 40% nothing. A won chest goes to Open Now.

7Jackpots (crit hits)

Every time a working rig's cooldown comes round, it rolls its crit chance. On a hit, it picks one of four tiers. Each tier pays a percentage of a 20% slice of the jackpot pool at that moment:

TierHow often (of hits)Pays (Earth)Pays (Moon, 3×)
Pay Dirt50%1% of the slice3%
Strike30%2%6%
Bonanza15%5%15%
Mother Lode5%10%30%

The jackpot pool grows with the 25% share of every day's emissions and shrinks as hits are paid. Rigs only roll while they are still working, so a finished rig sitting in the ground rolls nothing.

It's blind on purpose. You never see what a rig has struck while it's down there. The reveal screen when you pull is the payoff. On the Moon, the finds are real lunar minerals: Olivine Shard, Titanium Ore, Platinum Nugget and Tranquillityite Crystal.

8The Moon

  • Its five landing spots are real lunar craters: Cabeus, Haworth, Shoemaker, Faustini and Shackleton.
  • Every jackpot pays 3×.
  • The three Moon rigs (PRIME-1, VIPER, RASSOR) work at full strength and can only work on the Moon.
  • Any Earth rig can be sent up, but at half Mining Power and half roll speed (its cooldown doubles). Its crit chance is unchanged.
  • No boosts work on the Moon.
  • The Moon has no heat. It always gets a fixed 20% of daily emissions, shared between the rigs working there by mining power. The four Earth sites split the other 80% by heat.
  • Moon rigs come from chests: rarely from MINE IT and MYSTERY, far more often from HIGH GRADE.

9Boosts and shields

Boosts

A boost makes a rig work faster, never harder. It speeds up what's left of the cycle and shortens the crit cooldown by the same amount. Mining Power, crit chance and jackpot size are unchanged.

BoostRaritySpeed
Sharpened BitCommon×1.25
High-Octane FuelUncommon×2
Turbo Piston KitRare×5
Diamond-Tipped HeadEpic×10
Overdrive CoreLegendary×25
  • A boost lasts 24 hours, or until the rig is pulled.
  • One boost per rig at a time. You can't boost a finished rig, and boosts don't work on the Moon.
  • A boosted rig finishes sooner, so it spends less time in the stream. The trade-off: you free the slot for the next rig sooner.

Shields

A shield stops anyone raiding you while it's up. There are three: 8-Hour (Rare), 12-Hour (Epic) and 24-Hour (Legendary). Shields come from MYSTERY chests or the marketplace. Use one from Bag → Items. A second shield adds its hours on top of one that's already running.

Attacking drops your shield. The moment you start a raid, your own shield is gone, with no refund.

10Claiming your earnings

The bar at the top shows Mined, unclaimed. It grows every second while your rigs work. Tap Claim to move it to your wallet.

  • You can claim once every 3 hours, timed from your own last claim. Nothing is lost by waiting: the number keeps climbing.
  • If you were referred (and every player is), 4.01% of each claim goes to the three miners above you. If your chain is shorter, the cut is smaller.
  • If you owe any raid fee (see Raids), it's burned from the claim first.
  • Your very first claim also creates your $GREEN account in your wallet. That costs a little SOL as account rent, which stays yours.
Unclaimed $GREEN can be raided. Claiming regularly keeps less of it exposed. Jackpot winnings and referral earnings are never raidable.

12Open Now

Bag → 📦 Open Now is where anything handed to you lands sealed: the daily free chest, pull bonuses, scrap rewards, raffle prizes, event drops and support grants. Open them one at a time or tap Open all.

Event drops also appear here under Ready to claim while their window is open. Claim them before they close; unclaimed drops are never handed out.

A sealed chest can't be sold. Open it, and what's inside is yours to use or trade.

What a level gives you

  • Each Earth site: one more free slot, plus the right to buy one more paid slot (0.05 SOL each). At Level 5 that's up to 10 slots per Earth site.
  • The Moon: one more crater you can open. Cabeus is free; the others cost 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1.0 SOL and must be opened in order.
  • Higher chest limits, more HIGH GRADE chests and more raids per day.

A level never hands you a paid slot; it only raises how many you can buy. SOL spent on levels, slots and craters goes to the liquidity pool (and part to the treasury once the pool is deep).

15The marketplace

Buy and sell rigs, boosts and shields with other players, priced in SOL.

  • You set the price, but never below the floor for that item.
  • 10% fee: the seller keeps 90%, 5% buys back and burns $GREEN, 5% goes to the treasury. The buyer pays all three parts in one transaction.
  • Listings run 7 days and can't be cancelled. If nothing sells, the item comes back to you automatically.
  • When you tap Buy, the listing is held for you for a few minutes while you pay, so nobody else can grab it mid-purchase.

16Raids and hired raiders

Raid another miner to take part of the $GREEN they haven't claimed yet. You need at least one rig deployed: raids are your Mining Power against theirs.

How a raid works

  • Targets are real miners with between 0.4× and 1.7× your Mining Power who aren't shielded. Tap 🔄 for new targets.
  • Win chance = your power ÷ (your power + theirs), but never below 10% or above 90%. There's no hidden defence: the number you see is the number rolled.
  • Prize: if you win, you take 20% of their unclaimed $GREEN.
  • Fee: 10% of the prize, burned win or lose. It comes out of your own unclaimed $GREEN, not your wallet, so you need enough unclaimed to cover it. If you claim before the raid settles and can't cover it, the rest is taken from your next claim or jackpot.
  • The server decides the raid the instant you confirm, and it settles on chain within about 15 seconds.
  • Daily raids: 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 at Levels 1–5.
  • A miner who actually gets robbed gets a free 3-hour shield. Your own shield drops when you attack.

Hired raiders

You can hire a raider to swing a second time. It ignores Mining Power completely, so a small operation has the same odds as a big one. You can hire each raider once per day.

  • Weekly, +40 each: list something on the market, run a raid, buy a chest.
  • Free track: 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 10 tickets.

Points only count after the real action succeeds. Anything you earned but didn't claim is delivered automatically when the season ends. Open it from your profile or Market → 🎫 Pass.

🎫 GLG Master Pass

One lifetime pass that covers every GLG game. It's one per wallet, can never be sold or transferred, and is paid in SOL to the treasury. The price rises as more are sold, and supply is limited; the Pass tab shows the live price. Perks: bonus packs handed out by the team across the games you actively play, a founder board channel, early access, and a profile badge. It's a supporter pass, not an investment: it pays no yield.

20Getting help

Open your profile (👤, top right) and tap 🎫 Get help. Pick a category and describe what happened. Your last few actions are attached automatically, and you get a ticket number. If a payment ever goes through but the item doesn't arrive, the game shows the full transaction signature and a Get help button that fills it in for you. Don't pay again; send the ticket.

21FAQ

Do my rigs last forever?
No. Every rig works one cycle and is consumed when you pull it. A finished rig left in the ground burns after 7 days.
Why did my earnings per hour change when I didn't do anything?
Your share depends on everyone's mining power and on each Earth site's heat, and both change. The pool also releases a little less each day as it shrinks.
Why can't I see what my rig has won?
That's the design. Jackpot winnings stay hidden until you pull.
Should I pull early?
Only if you accept the penalty: under 73.3% you lose every jackpot win; from there to 100% you lose a sliding share. Pull at 100% to keep everything.
Can someone steal my jackpot or referral money?
No. Raids can only take your unclaimed mining balance. Jackpots go straight to your wallet, and referral earnings sit in a separate pot.
Why can't I raid?
You need a deployed rig, enough unclaimed $GREEN to cover the fee, raids left today, and targets within 0.4×–1.7× your power.
Why is the Claim button showing a timer?
Claims are limited to one every 3 hours. Your balance keeps growing while you wait.
Can I cancel a marketplace listing?
No. It runs 7 days and comes back to you automatically if it doesn't sell.
Which network is this?
The current build connects to Solana devnet, the test network.
I paid and nothing arrived. What now?
Don't pay again. Tap the Get help button on the message (or Profile → 🎫 Get help) and include the signature shown.
