01 — Getting Started

🚀 How to Join

Simple Farmer is invite‑only — you need a code from an existing player to get in, the same way GREEN LIFE works. There's no skipping this step, but there's also no way to get stuck: a roulette button will match you to a real active grower if you don't know anyone.

1
Connect your wallet
In this preview build the wallet is simulated — tapping Connect just gives you a placeholder address so you can play through the whole flow. Nothing real is signed.
2
Enter your invite code
Type a code from a friend, or tap 🎲 No code? Generate a random player's code to get matched to an active grower automatically. The roulette shows 5 candidates — 2 are fixed slots set by the team and never rotate, the other 3 are drawn fresh from real active growers each time you re‑roll. Every player has a referrer — there's no "skip" option.
3
Pay the $25 entry (or redeem a free claim code)
A one‑time entry — 0.25 SOL at the game's $100/SOL rate — pay in SOL, USDC, or USDT. It mints your Level 1 farm, opens your Starter Pack immediately, and drops $5 of $GREEN (500,000 tokens) into your wallet to start spending. That $5 comes out of the $25 — it isn't extra. Got a claim code instead? It waives the $25 entirely and you still get the same $5 bonus.
4
Watch your Starter Pack open itself
The moment your farm mints, your one free Starter Pack opens automatically — a guaranteed crop card, so you always have something to plant on day one. No sealed pack to go hunt down.
5
Plant it and start earning
Your Level 1 farm starts with 10 free plots already unlocked across four garden tiers. Head to Garden, tap your card, and it starts earning $GREEN immediately.
What "$5 of $GREEN" actually means: at the game's default $GREEN price ($0.00001), $5 is exactly 500,000 tokens — enough to buy your first few Seed or Mystery packs while your first crop is still growing.

02 — The Platform

🔗 Simple Farmer & the GLG Platform

Simple Farmer is one game on the GreenLifeGame (GLG) platform — a family of separate Solana play‑to‑earn games that share exactly one thing: the $GREEN token and its liquidity pool. Nothing else crosses over. Simple Farmer's sister game is GREEN LIFE, a much deeper cannabis‑farming game — different cards, different mechanics, its own farm, its own referral tree. The two games never share code, and progress in one never touches the other.

Where GREEN LIFE is the platform's full‑depth game, Simple Farmer is deliberately the simple, easy‑going one: fewer systems, a gentler economy, built for players who want to plant, wait, and harvest without a wall of mechanics to learn first.

Why that matters for your wallet: $GREEN earned or spent in Simple Farmer is the exact same token you'd hold in GREEN LIFE — same price, same pool. Farming here shrinks the same total supply everyone else's play shrinks too.
03 — How You Earn

💰 How $GREEN Gets Into Your Wallet

Simple Farmer pays out of its own dedicated rewards pool — 200 billion $GREEN, Simple Farmer's slice of a fixed 1 trillion supply. Each GLG game gets its own emissions pool (Simple Farmer 200B · Mother Lode 200B · GREEN LIFE 250B · Dino Island 250B); the liquidity pool, treasury and giveaway buckets are platform-level and shared by all of them. Every 24 hours, a slice of whatever's left in that pool — 0.05% a day — is split between every farm by its share of total planted power. That's a geometric decay, so the pool trickles for years instead of running dry: taking a percentage of a shrinking number shrinks a little slower every day.

The rate is paired to the pool size on purpose. Simple Farmer and Mother Lode run 0.05%/day on a 200B pool; the heavier games, GREEN LIFE and Dino Island, run 0.069%/day on 250B — they earn a little more because they ask a lot more of your time. The dial can move — up or down — but it is hard‑capped at 2% a day, on‑chain, forever. It can never mint a new token; it only changes how fast the same fixed 200B pool pays out.

Farm power is the number that decides your slice — every crop you plant adds its power to your total. But only crops that are still growing count toward your live earning share: the moment a crop finishes its cycle, it stops earning until you harvest it — even though it still counts on the leaderboard.

Your earn rate ≈ (your earning power ÷ everyone's earning power) × today's pool release. A bigger garden multiplier, more plots, and stronger crops all grow your share — but remember: raising everyone's power raises the denominator too, so the real levers on total payout are the pool size and the daily rate, not any one crop's stats.

Two Income Streams — the 70/30 Split

Every second you earn, it splits into two buckets that always add to 100%:

StreamYour CutWhen You Get It
Instant balance70%Streams every second into your Current Estimate. Claim any time — always safe, even from a plant you later pull early.
Harvest pot30%Builds up inside the specific plant that earned it, and only pays out when you harvest that plant.
Claiming has a floor. The Claim button stays greyed out below 1 $GREEN unclaimed — under that, every display in the game would round the number down to "0" anyway, so there's nothing meaningful to sign for yet.

The Five Garden Tiers

Every garden tier has a power multiplier. Here's the one rule that matters most: a ×N garden multiplies a crop's power by N and divides its grow time by N. Those cancel out — a single harvest pays exactly the same total in every tier. What a higher tier actually buys you is throughput: you finish that many more harvests in the same stretch of real time.

07 — Growing & Harvesting

🌾 The Grow Loop

Tap a card, put it in an open plot, and it starts growing the instant you sign. There's no watering in Simple Farmer — a planted crop grows purely on elapsed time, nothing to check in on until it's ready.

Planting Is a Two‑Step, Batch‑Signed Action

Tapping a card into a plot doesn't plant it right away — it goes in as pending, sitting there unsigned. You can stage as many cards as you like across every garden, tap a pending card again to pull it back out for free, and when you're happy with the batch, hit Confirm & Sign to commit everything in one signature.

Harvesting

Once a crop hits 100% grown, it's ready — and it stops earning the moment it does, so leaving it sitting there costs you emissions share, not just a payout. Harvest it (single card or in bulk) and you get:

  • The full harvest pot for that plant.
  • The card back in your bag, ready to plant again — a normal harvest never destroys the card.
You'll see a confirmation. A clean harvest pops a "harvested +N $GREEN" toast the instant it credits, so the exact amount is always visible on screen — not just a quiet balance tick you have to notice yourself.

⚠️ Pulling a Plant Early — Read Before You Pull

Every plant's cycle has three zones, measured as a fraction of that specific plant's own cycle length — not a fixed day count, since a garden multiplier can shrink a cycle by up to 100×.

Under 73.3% grown — the plant dies. Pull it now and you lose the card and the entire harvest pot. (73.3% ≈ day 22 of a 30‑day cycle.)
73.3%–100% grown — a sliding penalty. The plant survives and you keep the card, but the pot pays out on a shrinking scale — the earlier you pull inside this window, the more of the pot you lose. Wait to 100% for the whole thing.
100%+ grown — full payout, no penalty. This is a clean harvest — no sign‑off warning, no loss, card returned.

Whatever's forfeited on an early pull is burned, never returned to the pool. Your 70% instant stream is untouched either way — only the 30% pot is ever at risk.

08 — Boosts

✨ Boost Items

A boost card raises one planted crop's power for as long as that plant's own cycle runs — not a fixed number of days, since cycle length itself changes with garden tier. One boost per plant, no stacking, no swapping once it's on.

BoostRarityPower Bonus
🪱 CompostCommon+1%
👒 ScarecrowUncommon+3%
💧 Drip LineRare+5%
🐝 BeehiveEpic+10%

Boosts are deliberately gentler than a garden multiplier — the 1×–100× tiers already do the heavy lifting on power here. Get them from Boost Packs, the 30/40 split inside a Mystery Pack, or a failed crossbreed's consolation prize.

Don't boost a finished crop. A boost raises what a plant earns while it's growing — a plant already at 100% has stopped earning, so applying one there burns the card for nothing. The game blocks it and tells you to harvest first.
09 — Crossbreeding

🧬 Crossbreeding, Explained Simply

Pick two crop cards you own, pay a fee, and gamble on stronger offspring. Two rules that are easy to miss:

  • Veggie × Veggie or Fruit × Fruit only — you can never cross a veggie with a fruit.
  • Legendary can never be a parent — Epic is the highest rarity you can breed with. It can absolutely still come out of a breed.

Cost Table

You pay for both parents added together:

Parent RarityCost
Common$0.10
Uncommon$0.25
Rare$1.00
Epic$5.00

Example: an Epic + Common pairing costs $5.00 + $0.10 = $5.10. Paid in $GREEN and 100% burned — breeding, like farm‑level upgrades, carries no referral cut even if you were invited by someone.

Success Chance — Flat 50%

Every pairing has the same 50% success rate, whatever the rarity. That's deliberate — if breeding always worked, nobody would ever buy a pack. Fail, and both parent cards are gone, but you salvage one random boost card as a consolation.

If It Succeeds — Always 1 Card, Rarity Rolled

A successful breed hands you back exactly 1 offspring card. Its rarity independently rolls one of four branches:

BranchChanceLands On
Lower parent's rarity55%the weaker parent's tier
Higher parent's rarity25%the stronger parent's tier
One tier below the lower12%a step down (floors at Common)
One tier above the higher8%a step up (can reach Legendary)

Only the "one tier above" branch can ever produce a Legendary — and only when your higher‑rarity parent is already Epic.

Same‑rarity pairs breed better. Pair two cards of the same rarity and you pick up a real bonus toward landing one tier above your parents — roughly 33% combined odds of a one‑tier‑up result, versus about 8% for a normal mixed‑rarity pairing. Watch for it on the live Success‑chance preview on the Breed screen — it calls the bonus out whenever it applies, showing the true combined number rather than stacking it on top of the base chance.
🔥 Guaranteed‑breed promos. When one's running, any pairing of Common, Uncommon, or Rare parents succeeds 100% of the time — you'll see a green banner on the Breed screen and the Success‑chance row will read 100%. Only the success roll is skipped: what you actually get still rolls the normal offspring table above. Epic pairings are never included.
10 — The Marketplace

🏪 Buying & Selling

Every trade on the Market tab is priced in SOL, never $GREEN — that's where the fee split lives. A 10% fee applies to every sale: 90% to the seller, 5% buys back and burns $GREEN, 5% to the treasury.

Browsing the Market

Listings are grouped into one tile per item — real card art with its stats underneath, showing the lowest price currently listed and how many copies are for sale. Tap a tile to open a price‑tier picker: choose which price tier you want to buy into, choose a quantity, and it's a single signed batch purchase for the lot.

The floor is a hard minimum, not just a suggestion. You can always price above it, but the listing screen won't let you submit below it — the price field flags it in red and the list button stays disabled until you're at or above the floor for that rarity.

Tapping any item in Stuff → Crops/Boosts opens a popup with its full card art and stats, plus a Sell button — tap it and you're dropped straight into Market with that exact item pre‑selected and pre‑priced at the current floor, ready to adjust and confirm.
Listings can't be cancelled. Once you list a card it runs for a full 7 days. If nobody buys it, it returns to your bag automatically — nothing is lost, you just can't pull it back early to re‑price it. Only spare, idle cards can be listed — anything planted, staged, or otherwise in use has to be freed up first.
Honest note on this build: right now every listing you buy from is a simulated counterparty seeded by the game, not a real other player. The 10%/5%/5% split above is the real, permanent design — but in this preview, a buy's fee currently books to the treasury as one number since there's no real seller to pay a 90% share to. That changes the moment real players are trading against each other.

The pot is every ticket sold, minus a flat 5% fee that goes entirely to the treasury — deliberately no burn split here, since a ticket is a wager pooled between players and handed back out, not a spend that consumes an item. That 5% is what funds future giveaways.

The pot can also grow without a single extra ticket sold — admins can drop a bonus straight into a pool's prize pot, $GREEN or SOL, no ticket bought and no fee taken. It splits proportionally among whoever's already holding a ticket when the round draws.
Honest note: ticket purchases and admin bonus top‑ups are now recorded for real, instantly, in a shared server ledger across all three GLG games — a ticket bought here and one bought in another GLG game land in the literal same pool. What you actually see, though — the countdown, the winner, the payout — still runs on this device: bot tickets fill the rest of the pool and the draw itself is resolved client‑side. Real money must never enter a client‑resolved random draw, so that last piece waits on the real draw going live.

13 — Referrals

👥 Invite & Referrals, Explained Simply

Every player has a referrer — entry is invite‑only, so the whole game is one connected tree with no orphans. Your own code is minted the moment your farm mints, and it's yours forever. An invite code field accepts up to 12 characters, so there's room to use something readable — your own name or handle — instead of a random string.

The 3‑Level Earnings Table

LevelWhoYour Cut
1Someone you invited directly3%
2Someone they invited1%
3One level deeper still0.01%

It stops dead at 3 levels — a 4th‑level ancestor gets nothing, ever. This cut is carved out of $GREEN that's already moving (claims, harvests, and $GREEN‑paid pack purchases), so it never mints a new token. It applies only when you were actually referred by someone — an organic player pays nothing extra.

Referral earnings sit in their own separate pot — never mixed into your farmed $GREEN balance. Find your code, your invite link, and your unclaimed referral earnings under Stuff → 👥 Invite.

Breeding and farm‑level upgrades are exempt from the referral cut entirely — those always burn 100%, no slice taken, whether or not you were invited.

Event windows — how a new player competes with a 3‑month veteran

Admin can start a timed event on any board (or several at once — say, Rarest Collection and Marketplace Mogul running together as one themed weekend). The moment an event starts, everyone's counter for that board resets to zero for the duration of the event only — your lifetime total keeps counting in the background, untouched, and comes right back once the event ends. While an event is live, the board shows a 🔥 EVENT LIVE banner with the event's name and how long it's been running, sitting right above the regular lifetime board.

Because the event score is "how much you've gained since the event started," a player who joined 3 months ago and a player who joined this morning start that event on exactly equal footing — lifetime totals never carry over into an event window.
Honest note: rival scores on every board are simulated for now — there's no backend ranking service yet, so you're competing against a plausible-looking simulated field rather than every real Simple Farmer player. Your own score is always the real one.

❓ FAQ & Tips for New Farmers

Do I need to water my crops?

No. Unlike some GLG games, Simple Farmer has no watering mechanic at all — a planted crop grows purely on elapsed time. Just remember to harvest once it's ready, since a finished crop stops earning.

What's the single biggest new‑player mistake?

Pulling a plant before it's grown 73.3% of its cycle. That's an outright loss of the card and the entire harvest pot — always let a plant reach at least that mark, and reach 100% if you want the full payout.

Should I plant in the biggest garden tier right away?

Not necessarily. Remember: any tier pays the same total per harvest — the multiplier only changes how many harvests you fit into a week. A 100X Toxic Area plot is a bigger up‑front SOL cost for one crop's worth of speed; a handful of cheap 2X or 5X plots often gets you more total throughput for the same money.

Can I list a card I just planted?

No — only idle cards sitting in your bag can be listed. Harvest or unstage it first.

What happens if I don't have anything to breed with?

Grab a Seed Pack, your free daily Mystery Pack, or buy a spare on the Market — you always need two same‑category (veggie or fruit) cards to breed, and neither can be Legendary.

Is any of this backed by real money right now?

No — see the note at the very top of this guide. This build's wallet, chain, and marketplace counterparties are all simulated. Every price, odds table, and mechanic here is the real design, but nothing you earn or spend in this preview is real $GREEN or real SOL yet.

Quick Tips

  • Fill every free plot first. Your 10 starter plots are free — plant something in all of them before spending SOL on more ground.
  • Work your Season Pass Free track every day. It's where the old free-daily-pack habit belongs now — check it each login for what's earnable from that day's play.
  • Don't hoard low‑tier crops. A spare Common or Uncommon is perfect breeding fuel — the fee is small and a fail only costs you a boost card's worth of value.
  • Watch the "ready" state. A finished crop stops earning the instant it hits 100% — harvest promptly instead of letting it sit.
  • Invite people you actually expect to play. The referral cut only pays out on real activity — claims, harvests, and pack buys — so an inactive invite earns you nothing.
