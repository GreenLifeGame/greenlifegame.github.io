🔒 Minted once, never again

The mint authority is revoked immediately after launch. Not a promise — the network itself rejects any further mint.

🔥 Spending burns it

Every $GREEN spent inside any GLG game is destroyed. Supply only ever moves one direction.

🚫 No sell tax

Classic SPL token, no transfer fee. A tax was considered and dropped — it can't be sell-only, so it would hit buys and payouts too.

📉 Pools decay, never refill

Each game's pool releases a small share of what's left each day, so it tapers instead of running dry on a cliff.

💱 SOL, USDC or USDT

Every SOL-priced purchase can be paid in any of the three — stablecoins auto-swap to SOL in the same transaction via Jupiter. Whichever you pay with, it lands in the same place: the LP Curve, below.

💧 The LP Curve — depth first, then buyback-burn

Every purchase's SOL is paired with $GREEN pulled from the platform's shared 50B LP Reserve, at the pool's live ratio, and added straight to the real on-chain pool. That's a balanced deposit, not a trade — it deepens the pool without moving price. Where each dollar splits depends on how deep the pool already is, not market cap:

Pool under $25K100% → LP
$25K – $100K75% LP · 25% Treasury
$100K and up50% LP · 50% Treasury

Buyback-burn is separate and it's the one thing that DOES move price: every marketplace trade's 10% fee sends 5% back through the pool as a real swap — buying $GREEN with SOL and burning it on the spot — so trading volume itself is what pushes price up over time.

Bigger pools decay faster on purpose — GREEN LIFE and Dino Island ask more of a player's time, so they pay more. No $GREEN token exists yet and nothing here is for sale.
